About the Artist

Born at the foothills of the Sahyadri Mountain range in Satara, Maharashtra, Mr. Raut showed a natural talent and interest for fine arts from an early age. It was in high school when his gift was recognized by his art teacher who advised Mr. Raut to pursue a formal training in arts. Heeding his teacher’s advice, after graduating from high school, Mr. Raut set out for Bombay (now Mumbai). Here he earned admission to India’s finest art institution, Sir JJ School of Art.

At JJ, he embraced the opportunity with gratitude and dedication, refining his technique and strengthening the foundation that would support his lifelong career in the fine arts.

After graduating from JJ, Mr. Raut worked as a freelance artist to earn a living. Yet he felt a strong inner calling to do for others what his own teacher had done for him—nurture young talent. He decided to become a teacher and joined as an art teacher in BARC. Alongside teaching, he continued doing freelance work to support his family and provide a strong education for his three children.

Since retiring as a teacher in xxxx, Mr. Raut has continued to create art with the same passion. Even today, when he begins a new painting, there is a childlike excitement in his eyes. Over the past two decades Mr. Raut has made several paintings for his family and friends. These paintings now reside in homes across the world, cherished and proudly displayed.

In 2021, when Mr. Raut was stuck in USA during the pandemic, his family organized a small, socially distanced exhibition of his select pieces of work. Further down on this page you can watch few videos of this exhibition.

The purpose of this website is simply to share Mr. Raut’s life work, so people can enjoy his collection that he has created passionately. We do not intend to sell these paintings or seek any monetary benefits by publishing Mr. Raut’s work. Mr. Raut and his family would be honored to hear from well-wishers and admirers who feel a connection to his work.

We sincerely hope you enjoy exploring his collection and experiencing the spirit of this remarkably talented artist.
